About

A single path through design, validation, and launch.

Majid Dousti is a product engineer and technical program manager based in Dayton, OH (open to relocation). He delivers automotive, industrial, and consumer electronics programs from requirements, mechanical design, and validation through production launch, working with engineering, quality, supplier, and customer teams on scope, schedule, budget, quality, risk, and the critical path.

At Fuyao Glass America, he is a System and Quality Engineer on Ford windshield, sidelite, and roof programs, and built a live yield and defect dashboard for a 90-day Rivian Safe Launch as yield rose from 64% to 85% in two months — a mechanism later set up for other teams, where it remains in use. Earlier, he led two models in the AWRO reverse osmosis family at AmeriWater from material selection and prototyping through production release; owned product design and later program coordination at Meranti; and served as Technical Program Coordinator at Samsung’s regional office for the Samyar app launch.

He holds an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Wright State University (GPA 3.91/4) and is pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science there (GPA 3.8/4, expected Fall 2027). He is a Permanent Resident.
